## Key Theoretical Frameworks and Foundational Texts

### Homophily and Social Structure

- **Byrne, D.** (1971) - The Attraction Paradigm (foundational homophily theory)
- **Evidence in dating apps**: Political homophily, educational homophily, racial homophily all reproduced

### Social Exchange Theory

- **Thibaut, J.W., & Kelley, H.H.** (1959) - The Social Psychology of Groups
- **Application**: Users continuously reassess rewards/costs; constant alternatives increase comparison behavior

### Bourdieu's Habitus and Field

- **Bourdieu, P.** - Distinction; The Logic of Practice
- **Dating apps as field**: Capital (economic, cultural, social) distributed unequally; habitus shapes taste and desire
- **NTNU study**: "The construction of matches on dating platforms" applies Bourdieu explicitly

### Goffman's Dramaturgy

- **Goffman, E.** (1959) - The Presentation of Self in Everyday Life
- **Goffman, E.** - Interaction Ritual
- **Most cited in dating app studies**: Profile as front-stage performance; impression management

### Butler's Performativity

- **Butler, J.** (1990) - Gender Trouble
- **Applied to dating apps**: Identity performatively constituted through repeated acts; enables queer analysis

### Spectacle and Surveillance

- **Debord, G.** - Society of the Spectacle
- **Zuboff, S.** - The Age of Surveillance Capitalism
- **Dating app application**: Commodification of persons; behavioral surplus; data extraction from intimate relations

## Recent Research Trends (2020-2026)

1. **Increased focus on intersectionality** - race, gender, sexuality, disability, class
2. **Critical theory adoption** - Bourdieu, Butler, spectacle theory increasingly applied
3. **Autoethnographic turn** - researcher positionality and reflexivity emphasized
4. **Methodological innovation** - mixing ethnography with visual analysis, network analysis
5. **Global perspectives emerging** - research from Asia, Africa, Latin America appearing
6. **Algorithmic critique** - more focus on how algorithms enforce norms (heteronormativity, whiteness, thinness)
7. **Affect studies** - emotional labor, viral affects, dopamine economy
